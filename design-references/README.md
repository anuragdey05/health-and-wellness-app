# Design reference library

These files are selected references from
[`VoltAgent/awesome-design-md`](https://github.com/voltagent/awesome-design-md),
pinned to commit `f6961238d5cddcf8042a74a70fc400ec67181abb`.

They are inspiration modules, not six systems to merge wholesale. When the
product concept is chosen, use one reference as the visual spine and borrow at
most one clearly named discipline from another. Create the app's final,
original system in the repository root `DESIGN.md`.

## Installed references

### Claude - humane trust and calm reading

Use for warm cream surfaces, low-stress typography, compassionate copy, and a
human rather than clinical tone. This is the strongest starting point for the
app's everyday emotional baseline.

Source: [`awesome-design-md/claude/DESIGN.md`](awesome-design-md/claude/DESIGN.md)

### Mastercard - journeys, orbits, and soft geometry

Use for the experiential layer: circular progress, connected wellness moments,
guided pathways, and Anime.js motion along arcs. Its warm materials and orbital
composition can make progress feel alive without turning it into a dashboard.

Source: [`awesome-design-md/mastercard/DESIGN.md`](awesome-design-md/mastercard/DESIGN.md)

### Nike - energy and physical momentum

Use when the chosen concept involves exercise, sport, streaks, challenges, or
decisive calls to action. Borrow its scale, rhythm, and contrast, not its brand
identity or commerce patterns.

Source: [`awesome-design-md/nike/DESIGN.md`](awesome-design-md/nike/DESIGN.md)

### Apple - restraint and interaction polish

Use for focus, progressive disclosure, precise spacing, media-led moments, and
small press or transition details. This is a craft benchmark, not a reason to
make the product visually generic.

Source: [`awesome-design-md/apple/DESIGN.md`](awesome-design-md/apple/DESIGN.md)

### Airbnb - approachable social discovery

Use if the concept helps people find partners, groups, coaches, classes, or
nearby activities. Its friendly search, cards, trust cues, and generous spacing
fit community-led wellness flows.

Source: [`awesome-design-md/airbnb/DESIGN.md`](awesome-design-md/airbnb/DESIGN.md)

### Clay - tactile delight and ritual rewards

Use for authored illustrations, celebratory states, surprising transitions,
and playful micro-moments. Keep it concentrated around milestones and rituals
so the operational UI stays calm.

Source: [`awesome-design-md/clay/DESIGN.md`](awesome-design-md/clay/DESIGN.md)

## Recommended Anime.js interpretation

- Animate meaning: progress, state change, guidance, reward, and continuity.
- Prefer one signature motion language, such as orbiting, breathing, or flowing.
- Keep essential content visible before JavaScript runs.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and provide static equivalents.
- Avoid perpetual motion in reading, logging, or recovery-focused screens.
- Use quick tactile feedback for controls and slower motion only for transitions
  that help the user understand where they are going.
